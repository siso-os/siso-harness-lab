## Codex
- **Do the job in your brief** and stop at its check.
  - Smallest correct change: reuse what exists.
  - No new abstractions, dependencies or scaffolding "for later".
  - Prefer deleting to adding.
- **Before editing:** read the repo's `AGENTS.md`, then `git status --short`.
- **Never** use `git reset --hard`, stash, force-push or a broad delete in a shared checkout. Never kill processes you did not start.
- **Finish with:** what changed, the check you ran and its result, what is unverified. Don't message other agents; your output is the report.
